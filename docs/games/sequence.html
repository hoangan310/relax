Lap lai thu tu

Nhin cac nut mau sang len theo thu tu. Khi den luot con, hay cham lai dung thu tu do.

Dieu khien

Bam "Bat dau" de xem chuoi mau dau tien.
Do dai chuoi: 3
  1. Nhin mau sang theo thu tu.
  2. Cham lai dung thu tu vua thay.
